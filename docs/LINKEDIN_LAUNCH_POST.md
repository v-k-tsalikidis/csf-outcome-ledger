🚀 **Introducing CSF Outcome Ledger: A Local-First NIST CSF 2.0 & EU DORA Workbench with Live CISA Threat Intelligence**

Traditional GRC frameworks and spreadsheets often collapse complex security controls into arbitrary percentage scores ("78% Compliant"). In practice, the critical context—**who made the mapping decision, what evidence was evaluated, when it expires, and why it aligns with NIST SP 800-53 or EU DORA mandates**—gets lost.

To solve this, I designed and built **CSF Outcome Ledger**: an independent, local-first web application designed for transparent cybersecurity control mapping, evidence provenance, and real-time threat correlation.

---

### 🌟 Key Highlights & Engineering Features:

1. **NIST CSF 2.0 ➔ SP 800-53 Rev. 5 & ISO 27001 Cross-Mapping**  
   Directly links high-level NIST CSF 2.0 outcomes (Identify, Protect, Detect, Respond, Recover, Govern) to concrete technical controls (AC-2, IA-2, SC-8, AU-2) and ISO 27001:2022 clauses.

2. **🇪🇺 EU DORA & NIS2 Regulatory Overlays**  
   Built-in regulatory toggle mapping NIST outcomes directly to **Digital Operational Resilience Act (DORA Articles 5-14)** and **NIS2 (Article 21)** requirements for European enterprise compliance.

3. **📡 Live CISA Threat Feed (KEV API Integration)**  
   Integrates CISA’s Known Exploited Vulnerabilities API in real-time to compute a live **Sector Threat Severity Index** and correlate active exploits with required control remediations.

4. **🔐 SHA-256 Client-Side Evidence Provenance**  
   Uses the browser’s native **Web Crypto API** to calculate SHA-256 hashes of audit evidence files locally. Zero data leaves your device—100% privacy and cryptographic auditability.

5. **🕸️ Interactive Control Topology Map & CISO Board Report**  
   Visualizes the end-to-end dependency map (`CISA Threat ➔ NIST Outcome ➔ SP 800-53 Control ➔ Risk ➔ SHA-256 Evidence`) and exports a 1-click executive CISO report.
